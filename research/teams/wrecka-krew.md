## Archetypes
Seek & Destroy, Security.

## Selection (8 operatives)
- 1 Boss Nob: rokkit pistol + smash hammer, OR two rokkit pistols + choppa.
- exactly 2 Bomb Squigs.
- 5 from: Breaka Boy Demolisha, Fighter, Krusha; Tankbusta Gunner ('eavy rokkit launcha OR rokkit launcha; + fists),
  Tankbusta Rokkiteer (rokkit launcha + pulsa rokkit OR rokkit rack; + fists).
- each once except Bomb Squig, Breaka Boy Fighter, Tankbusta Gunner.

## Faction rules
- Wrecka Rampage: when friendly WK shoots/fights/retaliates, Roll Attack Dice step: +1 Wrecka point per retained 6.
  May spend up to 2 points (not BOMB SQUIG) to retain one fail as normal success per point. Max 6 points.
  Gain/spend any order in the action, but if at 6 at start, can only spend.
- Tanked Up: first time a friendly non-squig WK with Engage performs Charge, Shoot or Fight (not Guard) during each of its
  activations/counteractions, +1 APL until start of its next activation. FAQ: added at start of that action (before moving
  for Charge).

## Strategy ploys (CP not shown)
- Waaagh!: WK melee weapons Balanced.
- Destruction: WK ranged weapons Saturate.
- Tuff Gitz: when operative shoots friendly WK with Engage, re-roll one defence die.
- Amped Up: each friendly WK with Engage regains up to D3+1 wounds (separately).

## Firefight ploys
- Just a Scratch: attack die inflicts Normal Dmg on friendly non-squig WK → ignore that damage.
- Proppa Scrap: during BREAKA BOY or BOSS NOB activation, may perform two Fight actions.
- Demolition Job: after WK Shoot/Fight, before incapacitated removed, place Demolition marker in target's control range
  (primary target if Blast). Non-squig WK shooting/fighting/retaliating vs operative within 3" of marker may spend a Wrecka
  point for free (even with none). Remove in Ready step of next Strategy phase.
- Kaboom!: WK Shoot with Blast weapon: +1" Blast, Severe vs primary target. Not same action as Drill Rokkits.
  Severe doesn't gen Wrecka point.

## Equipment
- Drill Rokkits: once per TP, rokkit launcha / 'eavy rokkit launcha: lose Blast, gain Piercing 1 for that action.
- Engine Oil: once per TP when non-squig WK activated, ignore injured stat changes (incl. weapons) until end of activation.
- Extra Armour: non-squig WK −1" Move, +1 Save (improve by 1). Not cumulative with Portable Barricade Protective.
- Glyphs: choose Waaagh! or Destruction; first use 0CP, afterwards 0CP if you have any Wrecka points.

Weapons:
- Boss Nob: Rokkit pistol 6/5+/4/5 Range 8", Blast 1"; Two rokkit pistols (focused) 6/4+/4/5 Range 8", Blast 1", Ceaseless;
  Two rokkit pistols (salvo) 6/5+/4/5 Range 8", Blast 1", Salvo*; Choppa 4/3+/4/5; Smash hammer 4/3+/5/6 Brutal.
  - Wrecka Boss: gain 1 Wrecka point when it performs Shoot or Fight (not Guard).
  - Salvo*: select up to two different valid targets not in friendly control range; shoot both primaries in chosen order,
    then secondaries; no target shot more than once per action.
- Bomb Squig: Explosives 6/4+/4/5 Blast 1", Limited 1, Explosive*; Bite 3/4+/4/5.
  - Explosive*: can Shoot while in enemy control range; doesn't select target, it is itself primary target(?) — actually:
    "it is automatically the primary target and can't be in cover or obscured" — i.e. squig itself is the primary target
    point for Blast (blast centred on squig). Targets can't be in cover/obscured.
  - Stoopid: can't be given Conceal; only Charge, Dash, Fight, Reposition, Shoot; only its own datacard weapons.
  - Boom!: if incapacitated before using explosives this battle, roll one D6, or two D6 if you wish; any 4+ → free Shoot
    with explosives before removed.
  - Expendable: ignored for opponent kill/elimination ops (incapacitated and starting count), and for VP/victory
    conditions about escaping/surviving/being incapacitated by enemies.
- Demolisha: Tankhammer (bash) 4/3+/4/5; Tankhammer (detonate) 4/3+/* Lethal 5+, Limited 1, Detonate*.
  - Reckless Temperament: Normal Dmg 4+ inflicts 1 less on it; with Engage, Crit Dmg 4+ also 1 less.
  - Detonate*: first time this profile would inflict damage on enemy in the battle: that enemy takes D6+6 (2D6+6 if crit),
    and so does each other operative in its control range (separately). Action ends. Gain 1 Wrecka point +1 per operative
    incapacitated in that action. Damage can't be ignored or reduced.
- Fighter: Smash hammer 4/3+/5/6 Brutal.
  - BREAK STUFF 1AP: terrain feature in control range: if equipment, remove it; otherwise place Breach marker as close as
    possible to it in control range; within 1" of marker, parts of that feature up to 1" thick are Accessible.
    Not in enemy control range.
- Krusha: Knucklebustas 4/3+/5/6 Brutal, Shock, Smash*.
  - Armoured Up: when enemy shoots it, or when it fights/retaliates, opponent can't retain attack results <6 as crits.
  - Smash*: on strike, push enemy 1" in straight line ending further away in valid position, then move Krusha up to 1"
    straight line ending in that enemy's control range. If either impossible, neither.
- Gunner: 'Eavy rokkit launcha 6/4+/4/5 Blast 1", Heavy (Dash only); Rokkit launcha 6/5+/4/5 Blast 1"; Fists 3/3+/3/4.
  - Kompetitive Streak: once per Shoot, if shooting enemy already shot by another friendly this TP → +1 Wrecka point
    (checked at target selection; Blast secondaries count).
- Rokkiteer: Pulsa rokkit 6/5+/- Heavy (Reposition only), Limited 1, Pulsa*; Rokkit launcha 6/5+/4/5 Blast 1";
  Rokkit rack 6/5+/4/5 Blast 2", Heavy (Reposition only), Limited 1, Relentless; Fists 3/3+/3/4.
  - Pulsa*: no target; place Pulsa marker visible to it or on visible Vantage terrain. Marker gets 1 Pulsa point + 1 per
    attack success (max +3). Each operative wholly within X" (X = Pulsa points) takes D3 dmg (separately). Action ends.
  - Shokkwave: operatives within X" of your Pulsa marker: −2" Move, weapons' Hit worsened by 1 (stacks with injured).
    Each Strategy phase remove 1 Pulsa point; at 0 remove marker.

FAQ: Smash moving enemy — Fight doesn't end due to marker in enemy's control range only. Squig in Door/Hatchway Fight:
other operative is not a secondary target for Boom!, except within 2" & visible in Hatchway Fight with Kaboom!.

## Tactics (Goonhammer)
- Strong: 4+ save, 12/14 W, Rampage converts fails, Tanked Up APL 3 in combat, Amped Up healing + Engine Oil; Blast.
- Weak: direct/aggressive only; bad vs 14W elites; Gunners weak.
- Roster: Boss Nob (two rokkit pistols), 2 squigs, Demolisha, Krusha, Fighter x1–2, Rokkiteer optional.
- Equipment: Glyphs + Engine Oil must-take; Extra Armour situational; Drill Rokkits situational.
- Plays: squigs first to soften & gain points; Break Stuff to open paths; Demolition Job before shooting volley; Kaboom! +
  rokkit rack = 3" blast; Demolisha detonate on clusters.
