## Archetypes
Seek & Destroy, Recon.

## Selection (10 operatives, all 32mm, all APL 2, M 6")
- 1 Ironhorn: corrupted pistol + corrupted chainsword, OR plasma pistol + bludgeon.
- 9 from: Deathknell, Fluxbray, Gnarlscar, Gorehorn, Herd-Goad, Mangler, Shaman, Toxhorn, Vandal, Warrior.
  - Warrior: autopistol + bludgeon OR autopistol + cleaver. Only WARRIOR more than once.

## Faction rule: Frenzy
- Friendly FR without your Frenzy token would be incapacitated → not incapacitated, gains Frenzy token; all remaining
  attack dice discarded (incl. yours if fighting/retaliating); Conceal → Engage.
- While with token: can only be incapacitated as below; can't have Conceal; is injured; can't Pick Up Marker, unique
  actions (except Sweeping Blow) or mission actions (except Operate Hatch); APL counts as 1 for marker/area control
  (precedence over other changes).
- Incapacitated when: its activation or counteraction ends; enemy fights/retaliates vs it and strikes with a crit; enemy
  strikes it a second time with normal success (can be across two Fight actions); shot and Critical Dmg inflicted; shot
  and Normal Dmg from two or more attack dice inflicted; battle ends (before end-of-battle victory conditions).
- Opponent treats as incapacitated when it gains the token for VP scoring (Kill Op) and rules needing incapacitation.

## Strategy ploys
- Violent Temperament: fighting/retaliating, after rolling may re-roll ALL attack dice (not some).
  FAQ: if opponent's rule re-rolled/replaced a die, can still use — only for dice not re-rolled/replaced.
- Ambush: FR activated and order changes Conceal→Engage → ambushing that activation; while ambushing and fighting,
  retain one normal success as crit. Frenzied can't ambush.
- Pelting Firepower: FR shoots enemy shot by another friendly FR this TP → ranged weapons Ceaseless; if shot by more than
  one other friendly FR this TP → Relentless instead.
- Reckless Determination: enemy shoots an expended friendly FR and you can't retain any cover saves → retain one defence
  die as normal success without rolling.

## Firefight ploys
- Ruthless Rampage: after FR Fights and is no longer in enemy control range → free Charge (even if already charged),
  max 3" move.
- Wild Rage: FR activated → +1" Move until end of activation.
- Animalistic Fury: fighting/retaliating, strike with crit → +1 dmg.
- Bloodsense: during FR activation, it incapacitates an enemy in its control range → select one other ready friendly FR
  visible to and within 3" of the incapacitated enemy; when first is expended, may activate the selected one before
  opponent activates; then opponent activates as normal.

## Equipment
- Brass Adornments: once per battle, Animalistic Fury and Wild Rage each 0CP.
- Gore Marks: once per TP, fighting/retaliating: inflict 1 dmg on it and re-roll one attack die; if re-roll fails, 1 more
  dmg on it.
- Chaos Sigil: once per TP, when shot, start of Roll Defence Dice: worsen Piercing x by 1.
- War Paint: ignore Move reduction from injured.

  - Champion: can perform two Fight actions per activation.

  - Whip Control: enemies visible & within 3", if Herd-Goad not in control range of another enemy: their melee weapons

  - INCITE FURY 1AP: another friendly FR (not Shaman/Ironhorn) visible & within 3": +1 APL until end of its next

- Mangler: Vicious claws 4/4+/4/6 Ceaseless, Tactual Hunter*.
  - Tactual Hunter*: fighting vs expended operative, first crit strike → immediately resolve another success as strike.
  - Berserker: can't Shoot (Guard allowed but no free Shoot on interrupt). Pick Up / mission actions (not Operate Hatch)

  - Savage: first time it Fights each activation, if not incapacitated, free Fight after vs any enemy (overrides

  - MANTLE OF DARKNESS 1AP PSYCHIC: until start of its next activation / incapacitated: friendly FR visible & within 3"
    with Conceal and in cover can't be selected as valid target (overrides Seek, Vantage, except within 2").
    Not in enemy control range.
- Toxhorn: Autopistol; Cleaver.
  - Toxic Blessings: ignore APL changes; not affected by Shock; Normal Dmg 3+ on it → D6, 5+: −1 dmg.
  - Pox Bomb: can use stun grenades without counting toward limited uses; stun test result 3+ → also damage = die result
    halved rounded up.
- Vandal: Mancrusher 4/4+/5/5 Brutal, Vicious Blows* (Ceaseless when fighting).
  - SWEEPING BLOW 1AP: D3+1 dmg to each other operative visible & within 2" (separately). Not on Conceal. Usable while
    frenzied.
- Warrior: Autopistol; Bludgeon or Cleaver.
  - Warrior Frenzy: while with Frenzy token, can't be injured (precedence over Frenzy).

## Tactics (Goonhammer)
- Frenzy: aim to be "killed" during opponent's activation → second activation of value before dying at end of own.
- Strong melee, hard to remove; Ambush TP2; Deathknell War Gong; Mangler vs expended; Vandal Sweeping Blow.
- Weak: little shooting; Pelting Firepower niche.
- Roster: Shaman, Deathknell (+ portable barricade midboard), Fluxbray, Ironhorn, Herd-Goad, Gorehorn, Mangler, Vandal...
- Equipment: Brass Adornments best; War Paint; Gore Marks; Chaos Sigil situational.
- Combos: Bloodsense + Ruthless Rampage; Call the Attack to set up just outside enemy charge range.
- Against: kill Deathknell & Herd-Goad; stay outside charge range; crits finish frenzied; avoid giving them frenzy during
  your own activations (i.e. kill them in your activation = they come back on their turn).
