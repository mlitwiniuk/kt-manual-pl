## Archetypes: Infiltration, Recon
## Selection (12): 1 Shas'ui (leader); 11 from list: Shas'la (any number), Blooded, Transpectral Interference, Assault
Grenadier, Comms Specialist, Medical Technician, Weapons Expert (up to 2; ion rifle OR rail rifle + gun butt), Marksman,
drones: MB3 Recon (counts as two selections), MV1 Gun, MV4 Shield, MV7 Marker, MV31 Pulse Accelerator, MV33
Grav-inhibitor. Each once except Shas'la and Weapons Expert. Drone Controller struck out.
Pulse weapons: burst cannon, pulse carbine, suppressed pulse carbine, twin pulse carbine.

## Faction rules
- Markerlights: MARKERLIGHT 1AP: visible enemy gains one Markerlight token (max 5). Not in enemy CR. Once per activation,
  when token-bearing enemy performs Dash/Charge/Fall Back/Reposition, remove one token. Shoot and Markerlight can't target
  different operatives in the same activation (except MB3).
  Effects for friendly Pathfinders shooting it (cumulative): 1 Saturate + Balanced; 2 Hit +1 (max 3+); 3 can't be
  obscured; 4 Seek Light; 5 Seek. (Not fusion grenade.)
- Art of War (Shas'ui, Strategic Gambit if in killzone): Mont'ka: +1" Move to Pathfinders until end TP; Kauyon:
  Pathfinders with Conceal can perform a free Markerlight during activation. Each once per battle.

## Strategy ploys
- Recon Sweep: choose killzone edge (not own); Pathfinders wholly within 6" of it may free Dash, ending within 6" of a
  non-own edge. Not TP1.
- Suppressing Fire: enemy shooting a target that isn't its closest valid target can't re-roll attack dice (ignore
  Pathfinders on Conceal or obscured for "closest").
- Bonded: non-drone Pathfinder shooting/retaliating with ranged weapon within 3" of another non-drone → Accurate 1.
- Take Cover: shot, can retain cover saves → improve Save by 1.

## Firefight ploys
- A Worthy Cause: start of Firefight phase, one non-drone performs free mission action.
- Supporting Fire: selecting target within 6" of shooter, other friendly Pathfinders in that enemy's CR don't prevent.
- Saviour Protocols: non-drone targeted by Shoot → visible friendly drone within 3" becomes target (cover/obscured only if
  original had). Not vs Blast/Torrent. (0CP if MV4 is the drone.)
- Point-Blank Fusillade: non-drone not in enemy CR at start of its activation/counteraction, retaliating → may use ranged
  weapon (not grenades) as melee; can't block; Devastating/Piercing/Torrent no effect; if Engage & pulse weapon → resolve
  first attack die.

## Equipment
- Target Analysis Optic: once per TP, non-drone shooting target with your token → counts as one more token (primary only).
- High-Intensity Markerlight: up to twice per TP, a Pathfinder (not MV7) Markerlight → two tokens.
- Photon Grenade: 1AP once per TP, non-drone with Markerlight: visible enemy, D6 3+: −2" Move until end of its next
  activation. Not in enemy CR.
- Orbital Survey Uplink: once per TP, Markerlight can instead target any enemy in killzone (no visibility). Not with
  High-Intensity or Analyse.

  before opponent; max two in succession. Fearless on the Frontline: Markerlight allowed in enemy CR; Fall Back −1 AP.

  visible enemy −1 APL until end of its next activation. Not on Conceal / enemy CR.

  effects); Pulse carbine; Gun butt. Grenadier Specialist: frag & krak grenades without counting limits; Hit +1.
