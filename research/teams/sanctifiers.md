## Archetypes: Seek & Destroy, Security
## Selection (11): 1 Confessor; 1 Cherub; 9 from Conflagrator, Death Cult Assassin, Drill Abbot, Miraculist, Missionary
(entry 1: Ministorum flamer + brazier of holy fire, OR Ministorum flamer + gun butt + holy relic; entry 2: meltagun +

Salvationist. Each once except Preachers. A brazier option only once.

## Faction rules
- Blaze (weapon rule): attack inflicts damage via crits → target gains Blaze token (if none). When activated: D3 dmg;
  then controller either rolls D6 (3+ remove) or −1 APL for that activation to remove.
- Ministorum Sermon: Strategic Gambit: select one Sanctifier (must be Confessor if alive) → ORATOR until gambit used again.
  Sanctifier within 3" of ORATOR (6" if ORATOR is Confessor) benefits; if activated in range, benefits whole activation.
  Effect: Normal and Critical Dmg of 4+ inflicts 1 less.

## Strategy ploys
- The Emperor Protects: shot while benefitting → re-roll defence dice of one chosen result.
- Fervent Brawl: benefitting → melee Ceaseless fighting/retaliating.
- Zealous Persecution: fighting in activation it Charged → melee Lethal 5+.
- Rally the Flock: each Sanctifier (not ORATOR) benefitting may free Dash or Fall Back (max 3"), ending closer to & visible
  to an ORATOR. Not TP1.

## Firefight ploys
- Rosarius: attack die Normal Dmg on Sanctifier → ignore.
- Ardent Eradication: after rolling attack dice, Sanctifier shooting/fighting enemy within 3" of ORATOR (6" Confessor) →
  re-roll any attack dice.
- Redeemed Through Fire: Sanctifier with Blaze weapon incapacitated → each enemy visible & within 2" gains Blaze token.
- Unwavering Devotion: ORATOR or Miraculist targeted by Shoot/Fight → another Sanctifier (not Confessor/Miraculist/ORATOR)
  visible & within 3" becomes target. Not vs Blast/Torrent.

## Equipment
- Sanctification Orbs: once per TP, one Sanctifier (not Cherub/DCA/Miraculist) 1AP: visible enemy within 6" and each
  other enemy within 1" of it: D6, 3+ → Doused token. Blaze weapons vs Doused → Seek; remove Doused after such an attack.
  Not in enemy CR.
- Purity Seals: once per TP, shoot/fight with 2+ fails → discard one fail to retain another as normal success.
- Ecclesiarchy Texts: Ready step roll 3D6; if less than ORATOR's remaining wounds → +1CP (before Gambit, so not TP1).
- Imperial Cult Symbols: once per TP, shot while benefitting → change one of attacker's retained crits to normal.

## Operatives (APL2 M6" Sv5+ W7 25mm unless noted)
- Confessor W10 32mm: Mace of Censure M 4/3+/5/5 Brutal, Shock. Lead the Procession: from TP2, as ORATOR performing
  Charge/Fall Back/Reposition, before moving: each benefitting Sanctifier visible to it may free Charge/FB/Reposition max
  3", ending still benefitting. Commanding Declamation: once per battle, enemy would perform action visible & within 6":
