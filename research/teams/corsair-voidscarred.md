## Archetypes
Infiltration, Recon.

## Selection (9 operatives, all 28mm)
- 1 Felarch: (a) shuriken rifle + shuriken pistol + power weapon, OR (b) neuro disruptor + power weapon.
- 8 from: Fate Dealer, Gunner, Heavy Gunner, Kurnathi, Kurnite Hunter, Shade Runner, Soul Weaver, Starstorm Duellist,
  Warrior, Way Seeker.
  - Gunner: blaster OR shredder; + shuriken pistol, fists.
  - Heavy Gunner: shuriken cannon OR wraithcannon; + shuriken pistol, fists.
  - Warrior: shuriken pistol + power weapon, OR shuriken rifle + fists.
- Only WARRIOR more than once. Can't include both a blaster and a wraithcannon.

## Faction rules
- Rifles: shuriken rifle / ranger long rifle shooting in activation where it hasn't done Charge/Fall Back/Reposition →
  Accurate 1. Can still move after.
- Aeldari Raiders: each friendly VS can perform a free Dash during its activation.

## Strategy ploys
- Plunderers: up to D3 friendly VS each perform free Dash (order of choice). Each that does can't Dash during its
  activation this TP. Not in TP1.
- Piratical Profiteers: when VS shoots/fights/retaliates and it or the enemy contests an objective marker or one of your
  mission markers → its weapons Balanced.
- Mobile Engagement: when shot, if the VS performed a move action this TP → re-roll one defence die.
- Outcasts: VS more than 5" from other friendly operatives → weapons Punishing.

## Firefight ploys
- Opportunistic Fighters: enemy performs Fall Back, before it moves → 2D3 dmg per friendly VS within its control range.
- Light Fingers: during VS activation, enemies in its control range don't prevent Pick Up Marker or mission actions
  until end of activation.
- Capricious Flight: during VS activation, before/after action, it can Fall Back for 1 less AP.
- Contemptuous Adventurer: first friendly VS activated in a TP, if >5" from other friendlies, first time it Shoots or
  Fights that activation, weapons Relentless.

## Equipment
- Diuturnal Mantles: shot by Blast or Torrent weapon → re-roll one defence die. Not affected by x" Devastating x unless
  they're the target.
- Mistfield: once per TP, shot from >3" away, start of Roll Defence Dice: worsen Piercing x by 1 (Piercing 1 ignored).
- Runes of Guidance: once per TP, Way Seeker / Soul Weaver PSYCHIC unique action (not Warp Fold) +3" distance requirement.
  Not psychic weapons.
- Star Charts: Strategic Gambit, once per battle: roll D3; if higher than current TP number, +1CP.

## Operatives — all APL 2, M 7", Sv 4+, 28mm; Felarch W9, others W8
Common: Shuriken pistol 4/3+/3/4 Range 8", Rending; Fists 3/3+/2/3; Power weapon 4/3+/4/6 Lethal 5+;
Shuriken rifle 4/3+/3/4 Rending.
- Felarch: Neuro disruptor 4/3+/4/5 Range 8", Piercing 1, Stun; pistol; rifle; power weapon.
  - Veteran Raider: may perform a 1AP action for free via Aeldari Raiders instead of the Dash.
  - One Step Ahead: once per battle, after an enemy performs an action, if Felarch ready, roll D6; if higher than that
    enemy's APL, interrupt: free Shoot or Fight; can't select any other enemy as valid target (Blast secondaries ok).
    Then −1 APL until end of its next activation.
- Fate Dealer: Ranger long rifle (mobile) 4/3+/3/4 –; (stationary) 4/2+/3/3 Devastating 3, Heavy, Silent; pistol; fists.
  - Camo Cloak: shot → ignore Saturate; if can retain cover saves, retain one additional cover save OR one cover save as
    crit. Not cumulative with Vantage improved cover.
- Gunner: Blaster 4/3+/4/5 Piercing 2; Shredder 4/3+/4/5 Rending, Torrent 2"; pistol; fists.
- Heavy Gunner: Shuriken cannon (focused) 5/3+/4/5 Heavy (Dash only), Rending; (sweeping) 4/3+/4/5 Heavy (Dash only),
  Rending, Torrent 1"; Wraithcannon 4/3+/6/3 Devastating 4, Heavy (Dash only), Piercing 2; pistol; fists.
- Kurnathi: pistol; Dual power weapons 4/3+/4/6 Ceaseless, Lethal 5+.
  - Blademaster: can Dash in activation it Charged, only using remaining Charge distance, max 3".
  - Bladed Stance: fighting/retaliating, may resolve one success before normal order; it must be a block.
- Kurnite Hunter: Faolchú 4/3+/1/2 Rending, Saturate, Seek Light, Silent; pistol; power weapon.
  - Faolchú's Bond: first time each TP it retaliates (if ready) you resolve the first attack dice (defender instead of
    attacker).
  - Erudite Hunter (Strategic Gambit): select enemy within 9". Once per TP after that enemy performs a move action,
    interrupt: free Reposition (can't end further from that enemy) or free Charge (may change order; must end in that
    enemy's control range). Close quarters: ignore Wall for "further away".
- Shade Runner: pistol; Throwing blades 4/3+/2/4 Range 6", Silent; Hekatarii blades 4/3+/3/5 Ceaseless, Lethal 5+.
  - Blink Pack: Charge/Fall Back/Reposition can be warp jump: remove, set up wholly within 7" horizontally in valid
    position; unless Charge, not in enemy control range. CQ: measure through Wall.
  - Slicing Attack: Reposition by warp jump → draw line up to 7" from new to previous position, D3+2 dmg on one enemy it
    crosses (must have been visible at start of action).
- Soul Weaver (PSYKER, MEDIC): pistol; power weapon.
  - SOUL CHANNEL 1AP PSYCHIC: other friendly VS visible & within 6": +1 APL until end of its next activation.
  - SOUL HEAL 1AP PSYCHIC: friendly VS visible & within 6" regains up to 2D3. Neither in enemy control range.
- Starstorm Duellist: Fusion pistol 4/3+/5/3 Range 3", Devastating 3, Piercing 2; pistol; fists.
  - Quick on the Trigger: can Shoot while in enemy control range; then only enemies in its own control range valid.
  - PISTOL BARRAGE 1AP: two free Shoot actions (fusion + shuriken pistol, any order). Not on Conceal, not in activation
    it did Shoot (or vice versa).
  - FAQ: on Guard interrupt, can shoot normally.
- Warrior: pistol; rifle; power weapon; fists. Prowling Raiders: Capricious Flight and Light Fingers 0CP if specified
  operative is a friendly WARRIOR.
- Way Seeker (PSYKER): Freezing grasp 4/3+/1/2 PSYCHIC, Severe, Silent, Stun; Lightning strike 4/3+/4/3 PSYCHIC,
  2" Devastating 2; pistol; Witch staff 4/3+/3/5 PSYCHIC, Shock.
  - WARP FOLD 1AP PSYCHIC: two friendly VS visible & within 5": swap positions. If one did Charge/FB/Reposition this TP and
    the other is ready, the other can't do those in its activation this TP.
  - WARDING SHIELD 1AP PSYCHIC: friendly VS visible & within 6": until start of Way Seeker's next activation / target
    incapacitated / action done again: ignore first attack die Normal Dmg on it. Neither in enemy control range.

## Tactics (Goonhammer + synthesis)
- Strong: speed (7" + free Dash), tricks (psykers, warp jump, One Step Ahead), adaptable shooting + power weapons.
- Weak: 8W / 4+ fragile; 5" restrictions (Outcasts/Contemptuous) awkward in Gallowdark.
- Plays: dash out–shoot–dash back; hold One Step Ahead; Warp Fold swaps; Opportunistic Fighters vs Fall Back.
