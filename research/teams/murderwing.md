## Archetypes: Seek & Destroy, Recon
## Selection (6): 1 Chaos Lord (bolt pistol OR plasma pistol*, + lightning claw OR power fist OR power weapon; OR relic
lightning claws); 5 from: Champion (plasma pistol* or bolt pistol + power fist or power weapon), Curseclaw, Depredator,
Huntmaster, Shrieker, Warp Talon, Raptor (bolt pistol + chainsword OR plasma pistol* + chainsword), Skysear (flamer OR
meltagun* OR plasma gun*, each + bolt pistol + fists). Only Raptors more than once. Max two * weapons combined.
All APL3 M6" Sv3+; Lord W15 40mm; others W14 32mm.

## Faction rules
- Jump Pack: on Charge/Fall Back/Reposition, at start of any straight-line increment may BOOST if no part of base under
  Vantage: remove & set up within X" horizontally (X chosen, rounded up, counts toward action's move total). No extra 2"
  for Charge. Not under Vantage; not in enemy CR unless Charge. May continue moving. CQ: not over/through Wall, not
  through open hatchway.
- Boost actions: performed during other actions; one BOOST action per activation. BOOST ZONE = horizontal area between
  start and set-up location; enemies with base under Vantage outside it.
- Astartes: two Shoot (at least one bolt pistol) or two Fight per activation; counteract regardless of order.

## Strategy ploys
- Predators Above: ≥2" above floor → Balanced; when performing BOOST → Balanced until end of activation.
- Cull the Weak: Punishing vs enemy ≥2" lower, or with below-normal APL, or wounded at start of its activation/counteract.
- Nightmare on High: shot while ≥2" above floor or BOOSTed this TP → re-roll one defence die.
- Instil Fear: fighting: Normal Dmg 3+ on Murderwing → 1 less.

## Firefight ploys
- Malicious Narcissism: on your activation when only one friendly MW ready: until end TP, while you have fewer ready ops
  than opponent you may skip activations, until opponent has one ready. MW can't counteract until that op is expended.
  FAQ: Group Activation-type pairs can't be split.
- Murderous Descent: enemy ends Charge/Dash/Fall Back/Reposition within 3" horizontally and >2" below a MW (CQ: within 2",
  different room from start) → interrupt, MW free Charge ending in that enemy's CR; if impossible, cancelled.
- Long Forgotten Honour: resolving crit while fighting/retaliating: instead of strike/block, sequence ends, dice
  discarded, free Fall Back up to 3". FAQ: if Fall Back prevented, CP not refunded.
- Wings of Darkness: Fall Back/Reposition with BOOST → set up extra 3"; then can't Shoot, Fight or Carving Blow until next
  TP. Not TP1. FAQ: not for Warp Talon's arrival (4" limit).

## Equipment
- Bladefins: SLICE FROM ABOVE 1AP BOOST action: D3+1 dmg to one enemy in BOOST ZONE (during FB/Reposition after BOOST).
- Clawed Armour: CLAWED CHARGE 0AP BOOST action: 1 dmg to one enemy in CR, then Charge ends (during Charge after BOOST).
- Warp Fuel: once per TP, enemy ends Fall Back in its activation, MW in its CR at start → that MW free Reposition or Charge
  max 3".
- Vox-casters: VOX-CRY 1AP: each enemy within 2" stun test (D6 3+: −1 APL until end of next activation). Not on Conceal.

## Operatives (weapons)
- Common: Bolt pistol R 4/3+/3/4 Range 8"; Plasma pistol (standard) R 4/3+/3/5 Range 8", Piercing 1; (supercharge) R
  4/3+/4/5 Range 8", Hot, Lethal 5+, Piercing 1; Power weapon M 5/3+/4/6 Lethal 5+; Power fist M 4/3+/5/7 Brutal, Shock;
  Chainsword M 5/3+/4/5.
- Chaos Lord: + Lightning claw M 5/3+/4/5 Lethal 5+, Rending; Relic lightning claws M 5/3+/4/6 Ceaseless, Lethal 5+,
  Rending. Path to Damnation: starts 1 Damnation point; once per action attempt a Boon when its trigger occurs: D6 vs
  points: higher → resolve + gain 1 point; equal → nothing; lower → take damage = points, no Boon. At 6 → auto.
  Boon A: attack die inflicts 3+ dmg on it → ignore dmg = points. Boon B: fighting/retaliating, strike → +dmg = points.
- Champion: Chaos Champion (Strategic Gambit): move Challenge token to an enemy; fighting/retaliating vs it → melee gains
  one of Balanced/Brutal/Punishing/Severe/Shock. Path to Glory: kill token holder → +1CP.
- Curseclaw: Mutated claws M 5/3+/4/5 Rending. Frenzied Attack: incapacitated during Fight → strike with one unresolved
  success. SNATCH 1AP BOOST: enemy in BOOST ZONE; both roll D6 + Wounds stat; if yours higher → move enemy into BOOST ZONE
  or CR, no further from Curseclaw than it was; if in CR, FB/Reposition ends.
- Depredator: Great chainaxe M 5/4+/5/7 Brutal. Horrifying Dismemberment: kills while fighting → another visible enemy
  within 3" of either: −1 APL. CARVING BLOW 1AP: 2D3 dmg to each other operative (not only enemies!) visible & within 2".
  Not on Conceal, not same activation as Slice From Above, Clawed Charge or two Fights.
- Huntmaster: Power weapon. Pinned Prey: enemy would Fall Back from its CR (no other enemies in CR): 2D6 (1D6 if enemy has
  more Wounds), any 4+ → can't, no AP spent. STRIKE FROM ABOVE 1AP BOOST: 2D3+1 dmg to one enemy in BOOST ZONE.
- Raptor: Thrill of Flight: BOOST during activation → remove APL changes; ignore injured stat changes until end of act.
- Shrieker: Chainsword. Modified Vox-casters: enemies within 3" +1 AP for Pick Up / mission actions; enemy total APL −1
  at marker if one within 3". SHRIEK 1AP (also BOOST): visible enemy within 6" or in BOOST ZONE (must pick one in CR if
  any): D3 dmg and −1 APL until end of its next activation. Not on Conceal.
- Skysear: Flamer R 4/2+/3/3 Range 8", Saturate, Torrent 2"; Meltagun R 4/3+/6/3 Range 6", Devastating 4, Piercing 2;
  Plasma gun (standard) R 4/3+/4/6 Piercing 1; (supercharge) R 4/3+/5/6 Hot, Lethal 5+, Piercing 1; Fists M 4/3+/3/4.
- Warp Talon: Lightning claws M 5/3+/4/5 Ceaseless, Lethal 5+, Rending. Slice the Veil: may start in warp; TP1 when
  activated place Warp marker wholly in your territory, then expended; TP2 when activated set up wholly in DZ or wholly
  in territory contesting Warp marker, any order; that activation max 2 AP and 4" move.
