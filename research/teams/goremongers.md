## Archetypes: Seek & Destroy, Recon
## Selection (8): 1 Blood Herald; 7 from Aspirant, Bloodtaker, Impaler, Inciter, Skullclaimer, Stalker. Only Aspirant
more than once. Fixed weapons. All APL2 M7" Sv5+ 32mm; W10 (Herald 11).

## Faction rules
- Runes of Khorne: each friendly GM can't lose more than 8 wounds per Shoot action.
- Gore Tanks: each GM has Gore Tank: full / half / empty; starts half. Increase/decrease one level; not above full or
  below empty. Increase when it incapacitates an enemy within its CR, or visible & within 2". Decrease when it uses a
  Sanguavitae rule.
- Sanguavitae: requires decreasing Gore Tank. Same rule max once per activation/counteraction; max two Sanguavitae per
  activation/counteraction; Mania and Fury not both in same activation.
  - Rejuvenate: before/after action (activation or counteraction): regain D3+1.
  - Mania: before/after action during activation: +1 APL until start of its next activation.
  - Fury: before/after action during activation: can perform two Fight actions; second is free.
  - Rake: performing Charge, after move: D3 dmg to one enemy within CR.
  - Surge: Charge or Reposition: +1" Move for that action.
  - Rage: performing Fight: +1 Atk melee for that action.

## Strategy ploys
- Enhanced Violence: Gore Tank half → melee Balanced; full → Relentless. FAQ: checked after decreasing for Sanguavitae.
- Augmented Endurance: shot: half → re-roll one defence die; full → re-roll any.
- Gory Tenacity: fighting/retaliating, first time opponent strikes it in sequence → halve damage (round up, min 2).
- Hunt for Blood: select one GM; if Conceal → Engage; free Charge max 3".

## Firefight ploys
- Unbridled Aggression: GM that Charged this activation fighting, end of Roll Attack Dice → melee Severe.
- Gorethirst: when you would counteract: GM with Conceal can counteract, first change order to Engage; only Charge, Shoot
  or Fight.
- Destructive Demise: GM incapacitated, before removal: dmg to one enemy in its CR: empty D3, half D3+1, full D3+2.
- Lacerate Flesh: GM with empty tank activated or counteracts: increase tank; at end decrease (not Sanguavitae); if can't,
  D3 dmg to it.

## Equipment
- Gory Totem: set up in your territory >2" from other markers (except Cadaver). Enemies within 3" can't re-roll attack
  dice (shoot/fight/retaliate).
- Bloody Cadaver: set up in your territory >2" from other markers (except Totem). GM can Pick Up it. Ready step: increase
  tank of one GM controlling it (not in enemy CR).
- Chaos Sigil: once per TP, shot: worsen Piercing x by 1.
- Wrist Chains: once per TP, Shoot: chainblade, chainglaive, great chainaxe (ignore Brutal), pickrippers count as ranged
  weapons with Range 2". FAQ: Enhanced Violence Balanced/Relentless does NOT carry over (FAQ says no; Wahapedia text said
  still applies — FAQ answer: No).

## Operatives
- Blood Herald W11: Icon of Khorne R 4/2+/4/4 Range 8", Saturate; Chainblade M 4/3+/4/5 Rending. Khorne's Favour: once per
  activation, before/after action, if tank empty → increase. Impending Apotheosis: once per battle ignore Normal Dmg of one
  attack die.
- Aspirant: Autopistol R 4/4+/2/3 Range 8"; Chainglaive M 4/3+/4/5 Rending. Obsessive Bloodlust: once per TP, ends Fight
  no longer in enemy CR → free Charge (even if already charged), max 2"; doesn't prevent later Charge/Dash/Reposition.
- Bloodtaker: Autopistol; Ritual blade M 4/3+/3/5 Ritual (first time inflict dmg on enemy in its CR in sequence →
  increase tank; cumulative with kill increase). TRANSFUSION RITUAL 1AP: decrease own tank (not a Sanguavitae use) →
  increase tank of other GM within 8". Not in enemy CR / empty tank.
- Impaler: Autopistol; Fleshskewer (ranged) R 4/3+/4/5 Range 8", Piercing Crits 1, Stun, Drag; (stab) M 4/3+/3/4.
  Drag: start Resolve Attack Dice, move target up to 2" × successful unblocked dice toward Impaler (as close as possible,
  straight increments, ignore drop); then may discard any successful unblocked dice. GORE-FUEL 1AP: decrease tank; until
  it uses any fleshskewer profile, all have Severe. Not in enemy CR / empty.
- Inciter: Dual autopistols (focused) R 4/3+/2/2 Range 8", Ceaseless, Devastating 1, Rending; (point-blank) M? 4/3+/3/4
  Ceaseless, Rending. DASH AND SPRAY 1AP: free Dash + free Shoot (focused only), any order. Not Conceal / enemy CR.
  Incite the Hunt: incapacitates enemy >2" away → increase tank of one GM within 8" of that enemy. Bleeding: damages with
  dual autopistols without killing → enemy gains Bleeding token (if none); a GM within 8" of it, before/after action,
  may remove token to increase own tank.
- Skullclaimer: Autopistol; Great chainaxe M 4/3+/5/6 Brutal. Brutish: crit dmg on it may be normal. Claim Skull: once per
  TP kill with chainaxe → +1CP.
- Stalker: Autopistol; Pickrippers M 4/3+/4/5 Rending. Climbing Picks: climb counts as 2". Rooftop Stalker: Relentless
  melee in activation it dropped from Vantage ≥2", or vs enemy on Vantage ≥2".
