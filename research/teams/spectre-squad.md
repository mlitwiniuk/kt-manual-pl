## Archetypes: Infiltration, Recon
## Selection (11): fixed Veteran Sergeant + Vox-Relay Beacon; 9 from Field Medicae, Grenadier, Guide, Gunner (meltagun
or plasma gun; + gun butt — list says fists, datacard gun butt), Heavy Gunner, Loader, Sharpshooter, Stub-Gunner,
Trooper, Vox-Operator. Lasrifle OR lascarbine (+ gun butt) for Medicae/Grenadier/Guide/Trooper/Vox-Op. Only TROOPER more
than once. All 28mm (Beacon 25mm).

## Faction rules
- Elite Fieldcraft: Ready step +1 Fieldcraft point (+2 if friendly Vox-Operator in killzone, not in enemy CR). Discard at
  end of TP. After an enemy with Engage performs an action during its activation, spend 1 to interrupt: a ready friendly
  Spectre not in control range of other enemies performs free Shoot (not Guard), Dash or Reposition. Each enemy
  activation interrupted once (Guard incl.). Each friendly once per TP. Shoot: may change order; only that enemy valid
  target; being in its CR doesn't prevent; Guard point-blank rules apply. Reposition/Dash: can't end closer to that enemy
  or opp drop zone unless wholly in own DZ; that Reposition can't be repeated this TP; CQ ignore Wall, no teleport.
- Camo Cloaks: shot, can retain cover saves → one additional cover save or one cover save as crit. Not cumulative with
  Vantage.

## Strategy ploys
- Disappear: one Spectre free Reposition (can't end closer to enemies / opp DZ unless wholly own DZ; not repeat this TP).
- Hidden Engagement: Spectre shooting from cover (target's perspective) → Balanced.
- Ambushing Volley: Spectre activated >3" from enemies and not a valid target for any enemy before order determined →
  ranged weapons Devastating 1 this activation (not suppressive autostubber); if already Devastating x → x+1.
- Patience: choose: once skip an activation (only after opp's first activation); OR Spectre Shoot/Fight as last friendly
  activated this TP → Relentless.

## Firefight ploys
- Dodge: Fall Back for 1 less AP.
- Silent Killers: before/after action, Spectre with Conceal and not a valid target → can Charge on Conceal this
  activation; first strike +3 dmg, can't resolve other successes in that sequence.
- Sharp Reactions: after enemy with Conceal performs action, within 8" of a Spectre → can use Elite Fieldcraft to
  interrupt with that Spectre regardless of order.
- Prepared Defence: Spectre with Conceal or ready retaliating: resolve one success first (must block) OR one block can
  cover two unresolved successes. Not in activation where Fieldcraft interrupt used.

## Equipment
- Sniper Overwatch: once per TP, a Spectre may use ranged weapon 4/3+/3/3 Devastating 2, Heavy (Dash only), Saturate, Silent.
- Tvid-feed Triangulation: once per TP, selected target can't be obscured if also valid target for another Spectre or
  within 6" of friendly Beacon.
- Starshell Flare: Strategic Gambit: one Spectre free Stun Grenade action (not counted to limits). If it changes an
  enemy's APL, can't use this equipment rest of battle.
- Advanced Camouflage: 1AP unique action for Spectres (not Beacon): until start of next activation, while Conceal and in
  cover, can't be selected as valid target (overrides Seek/Vantage except within 2"). Not while visible & within 3" of
  an enemy.

## Operatives — APL2 M6" Sv5+ W8 (Sergeant W9; Beacon APL1 M0 W3)
Common: Lascarbine 4/3+/2/3 Rending; Lasrifle 4/3+/2/3 Heavy (Reposition only), Lethal 5+; Gun butt M 3/4+/2/3;
Fists M 3/4+/2/3; Laspistol 4/3+/2/3 Range 8".
- Veteran Sergeant: Scoped lascarbine 4/3+/2/3 Lethal 5+, Rending; Bionic arm M 3/3+/3/4. ISSUE MISSION 0AP: expended
  friendly Spectre (not Beacon) visible, not interrupted via Fieldcraft this TP → can be activated while expended until
  end of TP; then can't counteract this TP, can't Reposition if already did. Not in enemy CR.
- Vox-Relay Beacon: no weapons. Pre-Deploy: can set up outside DZ wholly in territory, >2" from markers & equipment
  terrain. Expendable: only Signal; no counteract/retaliate/assist; can't contest; ignored for kill ops and survive VP;
  can be moved through; enemy may ignore its CR; friendly Shoot may ignore its CR (not mutual). SIGNAL 1AP: other Spectre
  within 6": +1 APL until end of its next activation. Not in enemy CR.
- Field Medicae: Medic! (once per TP, other Spectre not Beacon would be incapacitated, visible & within 3", neither in
  enemy CR: 1 wound, free Dash into medic CR, both −1 APL, activation ends if its own). MEDIKIT 0AP: Spectre in its CR
  regains up to 2D3 (not one saved by Medic! this TP). Not in enemy CR.
- Grenadier: can use frag, krak, smoke grenades without counting toward limits; frag/krak Hit +1. Melta Mine: carries
  marker, Pick Up; Place → free Dash; not in enemy CR. Proximity Mine: first time marker within another operative's CR →
  remove, 2D6+3 dmg; if survives its action ends. Grenadier ignored.
- Guide: Prepared Killzone: +1 equipment option (Ammo Cache or equipment terrain). Scout Terrain: terrain in your
  territory or within 3" of Guide is scouted; once per activation each Spectre: ignore first 2" vertical of one climb on
  scouted terrain, OR Operate Hatch −1 AP on scouted access point. FAQ: stays scouted if Guide dies.
- Gunner: Meltagun 4/3+/6/3 Range 6", Devastating 4, Piercing 2; Plasma gun (standard) 4/3+/4/6 Piercing 1;
  (supercharge) 4/3+/5/6 Hot, Lethal 5+, Piercing 1; gun butt.
- Heavy Gunner: Laspistol; Missile launcher (frag) 4/3+/3/5 Blast 2", Heavy; (krak) 4/3+/5/7 Heavy, Piercing 1; Fists.
  Weapons Team: activated with friendly Loader in CR → missile launcher Heavy (Dash only) instead of Heavy.
- Loader: Laspistol; Fists. Weapon Assist: other Spectre in its CR shooting → re-roll one attack die. LOAD WEAPON 1AP:
  Spectre in its CR and not within 3" of enemies → free Shoot (not Guard), may change order. Not in enemy CR, not
  counteracting, not in activation where Loader or target did Charge/Dash/Shoot.
- Sharpshooter: Long-las (concealed) 4/2+/3/3 Devastating 3, Heavy, Silent, Concealed Position (only first Shoot in
  battle); (mobile) 4/3+/3/4; (stationary) 4/2+/3/3 Devastating 3, Heavy; gun butt.
- Stub-Gunner: Autostubber (focused) 5/3+/3/4 Heavy (Dash only); (suppressive) 5/5+/0/0 Heavy, Lethal 5+, Seek Light,
  Stun, Torrent 1"; (sweeping) 4/3+/3/4 Heavy (Dash only), Torrent 1"; Fists. Suppressive Fire: enemy visible & within
  3", Stub-Gunner on Engage, not in CR of another enemy → that enemy's weapons −1 Atk (min 1). Not if it Charged this TP.
- Trooper: Cool-Headed: once per TP, a friendly with this rule can Fieldcraft interrupt for 0 points.
- Vox-Operator: SIGNAL 1AP SUPPORT: other Spectre (not Beacon) visible: +1 APL until end of its next activation.
  Not in enemy CR. Also boosts Fieldcraft to 2 points.
