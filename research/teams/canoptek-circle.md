## Archetypes: Security, Recon

## Selection (8): 1 Geomancer; 2 Tomb Crawlers (twin gauss reapers + claws OR transdimensional isolator + claws; max one
isolator); 1 Accelerator; 1 Reanimator; 3 Warriors (gauss scalpel + claws & tail OR tesla caster + claws & tail).

## Faction rule: Obelisk Node Matrix
- TP1 Strategic Gambit: place 3 Obelisk Node markers wholly in your territory. Each later TP gambit: move each up to 3"
  horizontally. Nodes control markers within 1" that no enemy contests (if both players' nodes → none).
- Node within 6" horizontally of another → matrix between them (20mm-wide line, any height). All three → larger.
  FAQ: area between = only 20mm lines directly between; no through Wall; straight line only in CQ.
- Friendly CC operatives within your matrix: weapons Accurate 1; +1 APL (max 3).
- Nodes moving ignore Accessible/Obstructing/climb/drop but not through Wall.

## Strategy ploys
- Hypershielding: shot, matrix intervening or operative within matrix → re-roll defence dice results of one value.
  (Wahapedia: "re-roll one defence die result of one value" — i.e. any dice of one result? ambiguous; page says
  "przerzuć kości obrony z jednym wybranym wynikiem" — Ceaseless-like). Treat as Ceaseless-like for defence.
- Cryptogravitic Repulsion: once per action, first time enemy moves within your matrix, that move counts 1" longer;
  if not enough distance, can't move into matrix.
- Transdynamic Amplification: CC shoots, matrix intervening or target in matrix → Ceaseless.
- Souldrain: enemy within your matrix, or fighting/retaliating vs CC within matrix: −1 both Dmg of its melee weapons
  (min 2) until end of its activation/counteraction; moving through matrix → applies at end of that move.

## Firefight ploys
- Shield Flare: attack die Normal Dmg on CC, matrix intervening or in matrix → ignore. Opponent determines intervening.
- Nodal Response: during CC activation before/after action: change one strategy ploy used this TP (pay extra only if
  costs more) or use a strategy ploy now. FAQ: if cancelled (Absolute Authority) → other ploy or refund.
- Animate Obelisk Nodes: when activating/counteracting, move nodes up to 6" combined horizontally (round up).
- Sacrificial Thrall: Geomancer selected as Shoot target / to be fought → another friendly CANOPTEK visible & within 3"
  targeted instead (Fight: counts as in control range; Shoot: cover/obscured only if original had). Not vs Blast/Torrent.

## Equipment
- Matrix Manipulator: once per battle, during CC activation/counteraction, Geomancer counts as 4th node.
- Nanoscarab Caskets: whenever CC operative activated, regains up to D3.
- Awakened Obelisk Nodes: roll D3 after reveal; Animate Obelisk Nodes 0CP that many times in battle.
- Phase Shifter: once per TP, Geomancer shot: start Roll Defence Dice, worsen Piercing x by 1.

## Tactics (Goonhammer)
- Strong: matrix +1 APL & Accurate; area control; isolator deletes elites; returning expendable warriors; Geomancer
  tricks. Weak: node placement critical; 4+ hits; hordes; low count.
- Equipment: Awakened Obelisk Nodes best; Nanoscarab Caskets good; Manipulator niche; Phase Shifter situational.
- Plays: matrix over Crawlers; Animate nodes onto targets / block charges; Canoptek Control / Molecular Breach on Crawler;
  Accelerator in matrix.
- Against: stay out of matrix; kill Geomancer & Accelerator; spread vs isolator.
