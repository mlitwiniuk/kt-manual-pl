## Archetypes: Infiltration, Recon
## Selection (12): 1 Kill-Broker (Kroot rifle + ritual blade OR pulse weapon + ritual blade); 11 from: Bow-Hunter,
Cold-Blood, Cut-Skin, Long-Sight, Pistolier, Stalker, Tracker (each once), Heavy Gunner (once; Dvorgite skinner + blade
OR Londaxi tribalest + blade), Hound (up to 2), Warrior (any; Kroot rifle + blade OR Kroot scattergun + blade).
Base 28mm (Kill-Broker 32mm). Save 5+ all.

## Faction rule: Farstalker
- Strategy phase Ready step: change order of up to three friendly FK not in enemy CR.
- When you would counteract, may instead change order of one friendly FK not in enemy CR (counts as your counteract, not
  as that operative's counteraction).

## Strategy ploys
- Cut-Throats: melee weapons +1 Atk (max 5).
- Prey: shooting in activation without Charge/Fall Back/Reposition → Balanced and Severe (if already Balanced → Ceaseless
  and Severe). Can move after.
- Rogue: shot → ignore Saturate; retain one additional cover save or one as crit. Not with Vantage improved cover.
- Bound: each activation ignore first 2" vertical of one climb up.

## Firefight ploys
- Savage Ambush: ready friendly with Light/Heavy terrain in its CR selected to fight (i.e. retaliating) → you resolve
  first attack die.
- Poach: during activation, only needs to contest (not control) marker for Pick Up / mission actions.
- Slip Away: Fall Back −1 AP.
- Vengeance for the Kinband: friendly incapacitated by enemy → rest of battle, others shooting/fighting/retaliating vs
  that enemy Relentless. Can't use again until that enemy incapacitated.

## Equipment
- Piercing Shot: once per TP, Kroot rifle / scattergun / dual Kroot pistols (focused) → Piercing 1 for action. Not with
  Toxin Shot.
- Toxin Shot: once per TP, same weapons → Lethal 5+ and Stun. Not with Piercing Shot.
- Meat: once per TP, friendly (not Hound) activated not in enemy CR → regain up to D3+1.
- Trophy: once per battle, friendly (not Hound) not in enemy CR, before/after action → +1 APL until end of activation.

## Operatives (APL2, M6", Sv5+, W8 unless noted). Blade M 3/3+/3/5. Kroot rifle R 4/4+/3/4 (Kill-Broker & Cold-Blood 3+).
Kroot scattergun R 4/3+/3/3 Range 6".
- Kill-Broker W9 32mm: Kroot rifle 4/3+/3/4; Pulse weapon R 4/4+/4/5; Ritual blade M 4/3+/4/5.
  Call the Kill (Strategic Gambit, if in killzone): select enemy as mark for TP; friendlies shooting/fighting/retaliating
  vs mark → Balanced; if incapacitated, select new mark (repeatable). FAQ: with Blast/Torrent, new mark at end of action.
  Victory Shriek: mark incapacitated → select friendly within 6" of Kill-Broker: weapons Balanced rest of battle; each
  operative once per battle.
- Bow-Hunter: Accelerator bow (fused arrow) 4/3+/4/5 Piercing 1; (glide arrow) 4/3+/3/4 Silent; (voltaic arrow) 4/3+/3/5
  Blast 1"; Blade. ENERGISE 1AP: until end of TP or until it shoots bow, all bow profiles Lethal 5+. Not in enemy CR.
- Cold-Blood W9: Kroot rifle 4/3+/3/4; Blade. Hardy: crit dmg on it may be normal instead. Cold-blooded: vs wounded enemy
  → Lethal 5+; if also injured → Rending too.
- Cut-Skin: Cut-skin's blades M 4/3+/3/5 Ceaseless, Lethal 5+. Vicious Duellist: fighting/retaliating, each opponent die
  discarded as fail → 1 dmg to that enemy. Savage Assault: first Fight each activation, if neither incapacitated, free
  Fight vs same enemy.
- Heavy Gunner: Dvorgite skinner 5/2+/3/3 Range 6", Heavy (Reposition only), Piercing 2, Torrent 2"; Londaxi tribalest
  5/4+/4/5 Heavy (Reposition only), Piercing 1, Rending; Blade.
- Hound W7 M8": Ripping fangs M 4/3+/3/4 Rending. Beast: only Charge, Dash, Fall Back, Fight, Gather, Guard, Reposition,
  Pick Up Marker, Place Marker. Bad-tempered: enemy Fights and Hound is a valid target → may force enemy to select Hound;
  enemy ends Charge in CR of another friendly within 3" of Hound, Hound not in enemy CR → free Charge (may change order to
  Engage) ending in that enemy's CR. GATHER 1AP: free Dash or Reposition, during which free Pick Up / Place Marker.
- Long-Sight: Kroot hunting rifle (concealed) 4/2+/3/3 Heavy, Devastating 3, Silent, Concealed Position (first Shoot in
  battle only); (mobile) 4/3+/3/4; (stationary) 4/2+/3/3 Heavy, Devastating 3; Blade. LONG-SIGHT 1AP: until start of
  next activation, concealed & stationary profiles Lethal 5+, and hunting rifle targets can't be obscured. Not enemy CR.
- Pistolier: Dual Kroot pistols (focused) 4/3+/3/5 Range 8", Ceaseless, Lethal 5+; (salvo) 4/3+/3/5 Range 8", Salvo;
  Blade. Quick Draw: once per TP, enemy shoots it (or it's Blast secondary), ready → interrupt, free Shoot with focused
  pistols vs that enemy (may change order to Engage); must be valid target.
- Stalker: Kroot scattergun; Stalker's blade M 4/3+/3/5 Balanced, Rending. Stalker: can Charge on Conceal. STEALTH ATTACK
  2AP: free Charge (no more than Move), then free Fight; first strike → immediately resolve another success as strike.
  Not on Engage, not in enemy CR, must be within 1" of Light/Heavy terrain.
- Tracker: Kroot rifle 4/4+/3/4; Blade. MARKED FOR THE HUNT 1AP: place Pech'ra marker visible (or visible Vantage);
  friendlies shooting enemy with marker in its CR → Seek Light; removed at start of Tracker's next activation. FROM THE
  EYE ABOVE 1AP SUPPORT: other friendly visible & within 6" +1 APL until end of its next activation. Not in enemy CR.
- Warrior: Kroot rifle 4/4+/3/4 OR Kroot scattergun; Blade. Ready for Anything: once per TP during a Warrior's activation
  use Meat, Piercing Shot or Toxin Shot for it without counting toward their once-per-TP limit.
