## Archetypes: Seek & Destroy, Recon
## Selection (11): 1 Strain Leader; 1 Oversight Drone; 9 from Longsting, Shadestrain, Skyblast, Swarmguard, Warrior.
Only WARRIOR more than once. 28mm (Drone 25mm).

## Faction rules
- Communion: Ready step gain D3 Communion points (+1 if friendly Oversight Drone in killzone). Drones not affected by:
  - Shoot: friendly Stingwing can only target the closest enemy within 8" (excluding enemies in CR of other friendly
    Stingwings) unless you spend 1 point. Blast/Torrent: only primary must follow. FAQ: if none within 8", must spend 1
    to target one further away.
  - Charge: must end in CR of closest enemy it can, unless spend 1.
  - Pick Up Marker / mission action (not Breach, Operate Hatch): must spend 1.
  - Shooting: spend exactly 1 point to re-roll one attack die.
- Neutron Charge: weapons with "neutron" in name gain Piercing 1 when the Stingwing moved or used FLY (per fetch:
  "until end of the TP"; likely means for that activation/TP).
- Fly: moving action may FLY: remove & set up within Move horizontally (3" if Dash). No extra Charge distance. CQ: not
  through Wall, not beyond access point/open hatchway. Valid location; not in enemy CR unless Charge.

## Strategy ploys
- Hardened Exoskeleton: Stingwings (not Drones) fighting/retaliating: Normal Dmg 4+ → 1 less.
- Aerial Agility: enemy shoots a Stingwing while counteracting/interrupting, or in activation where shooter moved or was
  set up: one attack die that would inflict Normal Dmg → roll 5+ to ignore (only one die per Shoot).
- Airborne Predators: Stingwing moves/FLY during activation → weapons Balanced until end of activation.
- Sting: claws improve Hit by 1, gain Lethal 5+ and Shock.

## Firefight ploys
- Ocelli: shooting in activation where it used FLY → benefits of first and second main features of Vantage until end of
  action; treat as 3" higher for Vantage height checks. FAQ: doesn't affect targeting lines.
- Darting Flight: Reposition +D3" (or FLY set up +D3"); can't Shoot or Fight rest of TP.
- Neutron Overload: crit with neutron weapon in activation where shooter moved/FLY, target within 4" → +D3 dmg.
- Vicious Venom: crit while fighting (not Drones) → +D3 dmg.

## Equipment
- Neurostimulant: Ready step roll two D3 and pick one for Communion.
- Convergence Stimulant: once per TP Pick Up / mission action without spending Communion.
- Accelerant Stimulant: (not Drones) Charge or Dash +1" (FLY +1" set up).
- Aggression Stimulant: (not Drones) fighting → melee Ceaseless.

## Operatives (APL2, M6", Sv5+, W9, 28mm unless noted). Claws M 3/4+/3/4.
- Strain Leader W10: Neutron blaster R 4/3+/3/3 Devastating 2; Claws. Communion Helm: once per activation, spend 1 point
  (for free effect — i.e. counts as spending, e.g. for Warrior Instincts? actually "spend 1 point to gain nothing else")
  OR spend 1 point: another friendly Stingwing within 3" performs a free 1AP mission action (still pay Communion cost for
  it; Convergence Stimulant can't bypass). Commune: when selecting operatives choose one Stingwing strategy ploy; 0CP
  while Leader in killzone and not in enemy CR.
- Oversight Drone: APL2 M8" Sv2+ W5 25mm. Ram M 3/5+/1/2. Evasive Drone: only Aerial Guidance, Charge, Dash, Fall Back,
  Fight, Reposition; APL −1 for objective control (stacks); visibility from top disc; on Conceal & in cover can't be
  selected as valid target (except within 2"); ignore Piercing when shot. AERIAL GUIDANCE 1AP SUPPORT: until start of its
  next activation, other friendly Stingwing within 6" shooting enemy visible to Drone → Lethal 5+ and Saturate. No effect
  while Drone in enemy CR.
- Longsting: Neutron rail rifle (standard) R 4/4+/4/4 Devastating 2, Neutron Fragment; (aimed) R 4/3+/4/4 Devastating 2,
  Heavy (Dash only), Lethal 5+, Neutron Fragment; Claws. Neutron Fragment: target survives and any attack die resolved →
  gains token; when activated, D3 dmg per token.
- Shadestrain Sv3+: Neutron sting R 4/4+/3/3 Range 8", Devastating 2; Neutron grenade R 4/4+/3/3 Range 6", Blast 2",
  Devastating 2, Limited 1, Saturate; Claws. Ghost Rig: on Conceal, can't be selected as valid target unless shooter
  within 6" (not if only secondary). Camouflaged: shot → ignore Piercing, retain all cover saves as crits (not if only
  secondary).
- Skyblast: Neutron grenade launcher R 4/4+/3/3 Blast 2", Devastating 2, Neutron Bombardment (place Neutron Fallout
  marker in primary target's CR); Claws. Neutron Fallout: once per enemy activation, enemy within 2" of a marker → D3 dmg
  (not cumulative).
- Swarmguard: Flamer (standard) R 4/2+/3/3 Range 8", Saturate, Torrent 2"; (skytorch) R 4/2+/3/3 Saturate, Torrent 0",
  Skytorch; Claws. Skytorch: only via Skytorch Assault; no target — shoot each operative in torch zone (horizontal area
  between current & previous location), not wholly under Vantage; no cover/obscured; furthest to closest. FAQ: zone bends
  around Wall in CQ. SKYTORCH ASSAULT 2AP: free Reposition that must FLY, +2"; then free Shoot with skytorch only. Not on
  Conceal / enemy CR.
- Warrior: Neutron blaster R 4/4+/3/3 Devastating 2; Claws. Warrior Instincts: no Communion spent in that shooting
  sequence → neutron blaster Accurate 1.
