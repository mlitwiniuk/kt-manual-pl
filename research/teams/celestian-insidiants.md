mace); 8 from Abjuror, Censor, Cremator (up to 2), Denuncia, Mortisanctus, Reliquarius, Warrior (any). Others once.
All 32mm. APL2 M6" Sv3+ W9 unless noted.

## Faction rules
- Martyrdom: INSPIRING friendly CI incapacitated → before removal, select other friendly CI visible to it or within 6":
  gains one Benediction: Ardour (+1 APL rest of battle; not Superior), Wrath (datacard weapons Ceaseless rest of battle),
  Restoration (regain up to D3+2), Exigence (free Charge (max 3") or Dash, must end closer to the incapacitated one).
- Weapons of the Witch Hunters: PSYCHIC ranged weapons can't damage friendly CI; CI can't be selected for PSYCHIC action
  effects and never within required distances; within 3" of a CI an operative can't perform PSYCHIC actions/rules or use
  PSYCHIC ranged weapons; PSYCHIC melee weapons in that range lose all weapon rules and Dmg max 3/4. FAQ: operatives
  within 3" can't be selected/benefit from PSYCHIC rules at all. Anti-PSYKER: vs PSYKER → Lethal 5+.
- Inspiration: CI becomes INSPIRING when it incapacitates an enemy with 6+ Wounds, or when it performs Charge (before
  moving). While INSPIRING, weapons Severe.

## Strategy ploys
- Suspect & Eliminate: select enemy operative (it and each other enemy visible to it & within 2" gain Suspicion token
  until end TP) or a marker (each enemy contesting gains token). CI shooting/fighting vs token → Punishing.
- Suffering & Sacrifice: wounded CI shooting/fighting/retaliating → Balanced.
- Wrathful Determination: shot while on Engage → re-roll one defence die.
- Holy Resilience: INSPIRING CI fighting/retaliating: Normal/Crit Dmg 4+ → 1 less.

## Firefight ploys
- Glory to the Martyrs: CI incapacitated while fighting/retaliating → strike with one unresolved success before removal;
  if it kills, CI becomes INSPIRING before removal → may resolve Martyrdom.
- Unshakeable Pursuit: during activation ignore Move changes; if INSPIRING +1" Move (if became INSPIRING via Charge, use
  before moving).
- Faith & Fury: CI fighting strikes with crit → after resolving, D3 dmg to each other enemy visible & within 2".
- Fervent Hate: after rolling attack dice vs enemy without IMPERIUM → Ceaseless; if enemy CHAOS and/or PSYKER → Relentless.

## Equipment
- Psyk-out Grenades: select two utility grenades, both stun (if also taking universal grenades normally, can't pick
  stun). Stun test 3+ from CI Stun Grenade → dmg = die halved rounded up (PSYKER: full result).
- Saintly Relics: attack die would damage CI → roll D6 (two if INSPIRING), any 6 → ignore that die's damage. One die per
  action, two per battle.
- Vocifera Mortis: once per battle, Martyrdom target needn't be visible/within 6".
- Auto-flagellator: CI activated → may roll D6, take damage = half (round up); on 4+ becomes INSPIRING. One per TP.
