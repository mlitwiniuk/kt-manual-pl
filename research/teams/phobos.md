# PHOBOS STRIKE TEAM — Wahapedia kill-team3_ (version Oct '26; prev Nov '25). Official PDF eng_17-12 online rules.
Archetypes: Infiltration, Recon.
Selection: 6 ops. 1 leader: Infiltrator Sergeant, Reiver Sergeant (SI bolt pistol + combat knife OR bolt carbine + fists). Incursor Sergeant STRUCK THROUGH (not selectable in Oct '26). 5 from: Infiltrator Commsman/Helix Adept/Saboteur/Veteran/Voxbreaker/Warrior; Incursor Marksman/Minelayer/Warrior; Reiver Warrior (same weapon options). Each once except WARRIOR.
Faction rules:
- Omni-Scrambler (Strategic Gambit, if friendly INFILTRATOR in killzone): one enemy visible to a friendly INFILTRATOR or within 6" of VOXBREAKER; next firefight phase can't be activated/perform actions until opp completed activations = # friendly INFILTRATORs in killzone when used, or it's last to activate.
- Terror: enemy within 3" of friendly REIVER +1AP for Pick Up Marker and mission actions; marker control: total APL of enemy contesters -1 if at least one within 3" of REIVER (not a stat change; stacks).
- Astartes: 2 Shoot (at least one with bolt weapon = ranged weapon with "bolt" in name) or 2 Fight; counteract regardless of order.
- Multi-Spectrum Array: while friendly INCURSOR shooting, enemies can't be obscured.
Strategy ploys (cost not shown; assume 1CP):
- Guerrilla Warfare: ops gain unique 1AP action: change own order; not in enemy CR.
- ATSKNF: ignore injured stat changes (incl. weapons).
- Deadly Shots: ranged Balanced if not yet Charge/Fall Back/Reposition this activation OR target not in cover and >6". May still move after.
- Lethal Assaults: melee Balanced; if Charged this activation also Lethal 5+.
Firefight ploys:
- Patient Ambush: when it's your turn to activate, skip that activation.
- Critical Shot: bolt weapon, when resolving a crit: +D3 damage.
- Transhuman Physiology: shot, Roll Defence Dice: one normal success -> crit.
- Stealth Assault: Conceal op activated, given Engage, performs Charge then Fight; when resolving first attack die, immediately resolve another before opponent. No other actions before (may after).
Equipment:
- Purity Seals: once/TP shoot/fight/retaliate, 2+ fails: discard one fail to retain another as normal success.
- Additional Utility Grenades: select four utility grenades (can't also take universal Utility Grenades).
- Combat Blades: melee 5/3+/3/4.
- Special Issue Ammunition: once/TP Shoot with bolt carbine/marksman bolt carbine/occulus bolt carbine: Piercing 1 until end of TP.
Datacards: all APL3 M7 Sv3+ 32mm. Sgts W13, others W12. Fists 4/3+/3/4 for all.
- Infiltrator Sergeant (LEADER): Marksman bolt carbine 4/3+/3/4 Lethal5+. Tactical Advantage (once/battle each): re-roll initiative roll-off; use a firefight ploy 0CP on a specified Phobos op (incl Command Re-roll) OR Patient Ambush 0CP if ready & not in enemy CR. (Same on Reiver Sgt.)
- Infiltrator Commsman: MBC. Strategic Oversight: Ready step, in killzone & not in enemy CR, D6 4+ +1CP. Comms Array: once/TP during friendly activation/counteraction, change one strategy ploy used this TP at no CP.
- Infiltrator Helix Adept (MEDIC): MBC. Medic!: first time each TP another friendly would be incapacitated visible & within 3", neither in enemy CR: not incap, D3 wounds remaining, can't be incap rest of action; then free Dash ending in Adept's CR; both -1 APL until end of next activations; if during that op's activation, activation ends. Not if Adept incap, or if Shoot and Adept primary/secondary target. HELIX GAUNTLET 1AP: friendly in CR regains D3+3; not one Medic!'d this TP; not in enemy CR.
- Infiltrator Saboteur: MBC; Remote detonator 4/2+/5/6 Heavy(Dash only) Limited1 Piercing1 Silent Detonate. Plant Explosives: carries Explosives marker; can Pick Up it; when Place it, free Dash. Detonate: shoot each op within 2" of marker unless Heavy wholly intervening; no cover/obscured; resolve separately; needs marker in killzone; remove marker after; close-quarters killzones Lethal 5+.
- Infiltrator Veteran: Custom bolt carbine 4/3+/3/4: at end of Select Operatives choose up to two of Balanced, Lethal5+, Piercing Crits 1, Rending, Saturate.
- Infiltrator Voxbreaker: MBC. Voxbreak: enemy within 6" -> opp can't re-roll attack/defence dice for it. AUSPEX SCAN 1AP: until next activation/incap, friendly shooting enemy within 8" of Voxbreaker: no obscured; INCURSOR shooter gains Seek Light. Not in enemy CR.
- Infiltrator Warrior (WARRIOR): MBC. Vanguard: once/TP one friendly op with this rule Pick Up Marker or mission action -1AP.
- Incursor Sergeant (struck): Occulus bolt carbine 4/3+/3/4 Saturate; Tactical Advantage.
- Incursor Marksman: Stalker marksman bolt carbine 4/2+/3/4 Lethal5+ Piercing1. Track Target: Guard in any killzone; can Guard in Conceal but order changes to Engage when interrupting.
- Incursor Minelayer: Occulus bolt carbine Saturate. Haywire Mine: carries marker, can Pick Up it; can't place in enemy CR; removed if incap carrying & can't place. Proximity Mine: first time marker in another op's CR: remove, -1APL until end of its next activation, 2D3+3 dmg, if survives its action ends. Minelayer ignores.
- Incursor Warrior: Occulus bolt carbine; Vanguard.
- Reiver Sergeant (LEADER): Bolt carbine 4/3+/3/4 Accurate1; SI bolt pistol 4/3+/3/4 R8 Piercing1; Combat knife 5/3+/4/5; Fists. Grav-chute: climb = 2", drop ignored. Tactical Advantage.
- Reiver Warrior: same weapons (option), Grav-chute, Vanguard.
