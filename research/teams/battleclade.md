## Selection (10): 1 Technoarcheologist; 1 Servitor Underseer; 8 from: Auto-Proxy Servitor, Breacher Servitor, Combat

## Faction rule: Noospheric Network
- TRANSFER POWER: once per friendly Servitor activation, before/after an action, spend 1AP.
- NETWORK COUNTERACT: after that activation, counteract with one other friendly Servitor before opponent activates;
  select its order first; free 1AP action, max 2" move. Then opponent activates.
- APL below 2 can't use either. Doesn't require Engage; a ready operative can still activate later in TP. Each operative
  NETWORK COUNTERACT once per TP and can't also counteract that TP.
- FAQ: usable even if opponent has no ready operatives; counteracted operative can't go on Guard later.

## Strategy ploys
- Noospheric Possession: SUPPORT: Servitor within 6" of friendly Auto-Proxy or Underseer → Accurate 1. (Comms Device +3".)
- Duty of Reclamation: once per action, Command Re-roll 0CP if the die was rolled for a BC operative contesting an
  objective or your mission marker.
- Incantation of the Iron Soul: attack die inflicts 3+ dmg on BC → D6, 4+: −1 dmg.
- Prioritised Acquisition: select objective or your mission marker; BC total APL contesting +1 (if any contests);
  BC within 3" of it +1 Atk melee (max 4).

## Firefight ploys
- System Exorcism: when activating BC operative, remove one rules effect / stat change opponent applied to it (not
  wounds, injured, mission pack rules). FAQ: removes all identical tokens; no effect vs area-based effects.
- Remote Access (once per Tech-Priest activation): mission action without marker in CR if marker within 4" and no enemy
  contesting (enemies in CR of other BC ignored); OR Operate Hatch access point within 4". FAQ: not for placing markers.
- Auto-Ferric Supplication: Tech-Priest shot → ignore Piercing.
- Servile Surrogacy: Tech-Priest targeted by Shoot or Fight → visible Servitor within 3" targeted instead. Not vs
  Blast/Torrent.

## Equipment
- Covert Guises: roll D3; TP1 Strategic Gambit: that many friendly wholly in DZ free Reposition ending within 3" of DZ.
  Technoarcheologist only one Reposition in first Strategy phase (incl. Seeker).
- Electromantic Capacitors: melee weapons Shock; retaliating also Severe.
- Concealed Apparatus: once per battle, one Combat or Gun Servitor can use another weapon option from its datacard.
- Neurocyclic Reserve Cells: once per TP, TRANSFER POWER for 0AP.
