# Killzones (Wahapedia killzones page) — paraphrase
General: 30x22 unless stated; terrain features made of parts with types.
VOLKUS: 2 Stronghold, 2 Large Ruin, 2 Small Ruin, 2 Heavy Rubble, 3 Light Rubble.
 Stronghold: upper levels Ceiling+Vantage; door Accessible+Heavy; fire steps Vantage+Insignificant+Exposed; broken vent Blocking; 3 barrel containers on A Blocking+Heavy; small broken ramparts on A's Vantage edge Insig+Exposed; gap on B's lower Vantage Accessible; rest Heavy. B highest level: only one friendly op at a time (placed on one side). Control range: ignore door and parts <2" high for visibility.
 Large Ruin: upper level Ceiling+Vantage (same height as stronghold first upper level for lines); upper rampart Light; door Accessible+Heavy (ignore door for CR visibility); door viewpoint Blocking; unbroken windows Barred+Heavy (no visibility through unless op or target horizontally within 1" of window); rest Heavy.
 Small Ruin Heavy; Heavy Rubble Heavy; Light Rubble Light.
 CITYFIGHT: Condensed Stronghold: Blast/Torrent/x" Devastating gain Lethal 5+ when target wholly within stronghold and on floor or fire step (only target location). Garrisoned Stronghold: op wholly within stronghold retaliating vs one not -> defender resolves first. Door Fight 1AP: Fight action vs enemy on floor within 2" & other side of door active op touching; count as within CR; not in enemy CR / not touching door.
COMPOUND SIEGE (Volkus upgrade): 6 Stockade, 2 Bunker, 6 Fire Step. Stockade closed (Heavy) -> open once (breach point Accessible+Insig+Exposed). Open Stockade Door 1AP from inside. Shooting stockade: Hit 2+, extra defence die (4), Save 4+, ignore each 3 or less dmg; can be obscured not in cover. Fighting: no retaliate, Hit 2+, ignore each 5 or less dmg. 8 Wounds -> open. Bunker Heavy; inside op can Guard interrupting only outside enemies. Fire Step Vantage+Insig+Exposed. Fortified Position: visibility over Heavy only if op/target >=2" higher than bottom; windows block unless within 1"; cover on far side ignores Piercing; blast/torrent/devastating secondary same side; fight defender resolves first; climbing counts 4" vertical. Open breach point negates.
TYRANID INFESTATION upgrade: terrain for Typhon NPOs (Light/Heavy).
GALLOWDARK: 606x703mm 6x7 grid. Wall: Heavy+Wall; no move/visibility/measure over or through (except to killzone areas); for cover/obscuring only corners & ends intervene unless active op passed it. Hatchway closed (Heavy+Wall) / open (access point Accessible+Insig+Exposed; hatch Heavy+Wall; gap beneath Blocking). Operate Hatch 1AP: open/close hatchway whose access point in CR; can be done during Dash/Reposition and continue moving; not in enemy CR or if open and access point in enemy CR. Opening may put op in enemy CR -> ends move.
 CLOSE QUARTERS: Condensed Environment (Blast/Torrent/x" Devastating Lethal 5+); Guard 1AP (treated as Shoot; not Conceal / not in enemy CR); on guard until: performs action/moves/set up; enemy ends action in its CR and you don't interrupt; order changes; next TP. On Guard: once per enemy activation after enemy action interrupt, one friendly on-guard op free Fight or Shoot (incl. Hatchway Fight); can't counteract this TP; target chosen normally. Point-blank shot: target enemy in CR, Hit worse by 1, can't retaliate until end of that enemy activation. Hatchway Fight 1AP: enemy within 2" other side of open access point you touch.
TOMB WORLD: 606x703 6x7 grid; 1 sarcophagus, 4 debris, 16 half pillars... Portals = hatchways (Operate Hatch). Wall same as Gallowdark. Hatchway open: hatch removed. Breach Point closed Heavy+Wall / open (removed) Accessible+Insig+Exposed. Breach 2AP: opens closed breach point in CR; -1AP (min1) if datacard has "breach marker", "grenadier" or "mine", or has Piercing 2/Piercing Crits 2 weapon (not Blast/Torrent). Each enemy on other side with access point in its CR roll D6: 4+ -> -1 APL until end of next activation and dmg = result halved rounded up. Not in enemy CR, not if open, can't do for less than 2AP in activation/counteraction with Charge or Shoot. Teleport Pad: Exposed+Insig+Vantage; only one op; no equipment within 2"; ops on it in contact are in CR; from start TP2 friendly op on pad performing Charge/Fall Back/Reposition can teleport instead to other pad (swap if occupied); once per activation. Sarcophagus & debris Light. Close Quarters same as Gallowdark.
BHETA-DECIMA: 2 short, 4 medium, 2 long gantries, 1 thermometric condenser. Gantry floors Accessible+Vantage; pillars Heavy; connected gantries one feature. Condenser roof Accessible+Vantage; inner ledge Exposed+Insig; battlements Light; rest Heavy. Hazardous Area: no part of base can touch; floor op can't target floor target if 4" of hazardous area between; Vantage op can't target floor target (or vice versa) if a gantry footprint between (ignore gantry op/target on). Equipment can be set up on Vantage and within 2" of Accessible (override). Objective markers in BD not on killzone floor (mission pack).
OCTARIUS (Tournament companion): 4 ramshackle walls, 1 oil pump, 3 ramshackle barricades, 3 scrap piles. walls & barricades Light; walkway pillars Light+Vantage+Ceiling; oil pump upper level Vantage+Ceiling, horse head Exposed, small spigot Insig+Exposed, rest Heavy; wall door Accessible+Heavy (ignore for CR visibility); gaps Blocking.

# October 2026 update (WHC article 28.10.2026)
Tomb World special cover rules REMOVED: all parts work like regular terrain (e.g. pillars in wall segments no longer block as intervening). -> so Tomb World walls: no "only corners/ends intervene"? (article says special cover rules removed)
Vantage cover: new examples, Light or Heavy cover vs attackers below depending on rampart category & targeting line.
Ladders: can't boost op significantly beyond max span in single Reposition.
Breach in Tomb World: some teams previously limited to Operate Hatch can now Breach (team-specific).
No changes to Approved Ops / equipment. No new mission pack/killzone.
