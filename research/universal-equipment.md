# Universal Equipment (Wahapedia + official PDF w/ Rules Commentary June '26) — CURRENT
Select up to 4 options (mission pack), each option once per battle; pre-battle ones set up alternately one item at a time.
Ammo Cache 1x PRE: marker wholly in your territory. Ammo Resupply 0AP mission action on your cache you control: until start of next TP when shooting weapon on its datacard re-roll one attack die. Not in enemy CR, not on marker you don't control, not on marker already used this TP.
Razor Wire 1x PRE: Exposed + Obstructing; wholly in your territory, killzone floor, >2" from other equipment terrain, access points, Accessible terrain. Obstructing: crossing it within 1" -> +1" distance.
Comms Device 1x PRE: marker wholly in your territory; while friendly op controls it +3" to distance reqs of SUPPORT rules referring to friendly ops. Can't use opp's.
Mines 1x PRE: marker wholly in your territory >2" from other markers, access points, Accessible terrain. First time an op's CR includes it -> remove, D3+3 dmg to that op (any op incl. friendly).
Light Barricades 2x PRE: Light terrain, feet Insignificant+Exposed; wholly in territory, floor, >2" from other equipment terrain/access points/Accessible.
Heavy Barricade 1x PRE: Heavy; wholly within 4" of your drop zone, floor, >2" ...
Ladders 2x PRE: Insignificant+Exposed; wholly in territory, upright against terrain >=2" tall, >2" from other equipment terrain, >1" from doors and access points. Move through ladder as if not there (can't finish on it). Once per action, climbing terrain ladder is against: vertical distance = 1" if ladder within op's CR for entire climb.
Portable Barricade 1x PRE: Light, Protective, Portable; feet Insig+Exposed; territory, floor, >2"... Protective: op in cover from it improves Save by 1 (max 2+). Portable: only cover to ops connected to it and only if shield intervening. Move With Barricade 1AP: like Reposition, Move-2", no climb/drop/jump; barricade removed and set up connected after; not within 2" of other equipment/access/Accessible else not set up again. Not in enemy CR, not same activation as Fall Back/Charge.
Utility Grenades choose two (2 smoke / 2 stun / 1+1): unique actions, limited per battle per kill team.
 Smoke 1AP: marker within 6" visible (or on Vantage of visible feature); smoke 1" horizontally, unlimited height. Ops wholly within smoke obscured to ops >2" away and vice versa. Shooting enemy wholly in smoke: Piercing 2/Piercing Crits 2 -> 1 unless shooter within 2". Ready step roll D3: remove after that many activations or end of TP.
 Stun 1AP: enemy visible within 6"; it and each other op within 1" stun test D6 3+: -1 APL until end of its next activation. Not in enemy CR.
Explosive Grenades choose two (2 frag/2 krak/1+1): ranged weapons, limited per battle.
 Frag 4 / 4+ / 2/4  Range 6", Blast 2", Saturate
 Krak 4 / 4+ / 4/5  Range 6", Piercing 1, Saturate
Breaching Charge 1x (no setup): once per battle when friendly op performs Breach, -1AP (min 1), as if "breach marker".
